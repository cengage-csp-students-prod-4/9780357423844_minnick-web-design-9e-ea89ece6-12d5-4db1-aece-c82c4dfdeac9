Chapter 1, Extend Your Knowledge

Hunter Cabreira: 08/29/2026

Guideline 1.1 Summary: All non-text content (pictures, videos, buttons, etc.) should have nearby text to explain what they do, with the exception of decorations, formatting, and content not currently visible to the user.

Guideline 1.2 Summary: Pre-recorded video and audio should include descriptions and synchronized captions to help users better understand them, including those who cannot see or hear.

Guideline 1.3 Summary: Content should be organized in a way that is easy to understand and use, featuring correct sequencing, clear instructions, and clear controls.

Guideline 1.4 Summary: Make websites easy to read, hear, and modify by using clear color contrasts, allow text to be resized, avoid unnecessary background noise, make content work on small screens, and ensure pop-ups and other visual elements are easy to use.
