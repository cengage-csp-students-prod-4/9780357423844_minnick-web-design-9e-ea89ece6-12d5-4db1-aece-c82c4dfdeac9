How to Prepare for an Interview

To prepare for a job interview, study the job description, research the company, and practice clear answers to common questions.

Strong preparation also includes planning questions for the interviewer and confirming interview logistics ahead of time.

  • Research the company's background, culture, and products beforehand
  • Review the job description and match your skills to its requirements
  • Practice answering common interview questions
  • Prepare a few insightful questions to ask the interviewer at the end
  • Dress appropriately for the company's work environment and arrive early
